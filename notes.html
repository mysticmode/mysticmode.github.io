A personal notes to reflect for myself.

Notes on Ego:
  • One will not attain ego-less life if one's own life is the center of oneself.
  • Life is in a way is a dream. A dream that is well worth experiencing.
  • We close our eyes and we open our eyes and in the interim is what we do on what we think.
  • The ego is away when one dies to self. And when we die to the self the wholeness comes in.
  • The Yourness is not really yours but a manifestation. It is our duty to use our consciousness.
