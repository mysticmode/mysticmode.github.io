Writing Poetry [2026-07-15]

Writing poetry is like sitting between the sadness and solace;
In between this darkness and light,
there is a melancholic beauty.

That is succinctly visible as verses to portray.
just writing what they had been meant to write just as the way of
how they had been meant to see the world.

It's a silent beauty within oneself that can only be unravelled by honesty.
For the author writing to get done his cordial duty to let the
world see how human connections and feelings are so deeper than the depth of the oceans.
